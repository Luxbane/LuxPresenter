Powerful & Intuitive

Everything Your Church AV Team Needs

Designed from the ground up for high-pressure live worship services. Zero clutter, lightning-fast search, and dependable stability.

Smart Song Search & Typo Guard

Real-time inline suggestion with auto-selection. Type letters, preview updates instantly; typo-prevention stops invalid keystrokes, and hitting [Enter] directly pushes the slide live to the congregation.

Live Autocomplete • Enter to Live

True Dual-Display Output

Full separation between Operator Control Console and borderless Projector Output. Preview upcoming slides, manage service orders, and trigger smooth blend crossfades.

Independent Monitors • Zero Output Flicker

PowerPoint & PDF Engine

Present sermon slides seamlessly with the integrated LibreOffice Impress bridge and Google PDFium renderer. Full thumbnail generation and multi-screen projection of PPTX, PPT, ODP, and PDF.

Auto Presentation Cache • PDFium & Impress

Hardware 4K Video Player (libmpv)

GPU-accelerated video playback engine powered by libmpv. Supports 4K 60fps MP4, MKV, WebM, flawless video looping, low-latency audio sync, and zero dropped frames during worship.

Hardware Acceleration • Seamless Loops

Online Browser & AdBlocker

Built-in incognito Microsoft Edge WebView2 browser with adblocker support. Stream YouTube videos, live feeds, or online lyrics without popups, banners, or embarrassing video ads during service.

Edge WebView2 • Clean Ad-Free Projection

Nursery & Scrolling Ticker Alerts

Display urgent parent callouts or scrolling service banners directly on top of the live screen without interrupting the sermon or worship song. Full priority queue with customizable repetition.

Parent Pager • Live Ticker • Quick Overlays
