The lists are data files read by the program's own filter engine; they are shipped unmodified and attributed here.
"Update filter lists now" in Options > Online downloads newer copies from the addresses above into
%APPDATA%\LuxPresenterQt6\Data\AdBlock (or the portable Data\AdBlock folder). A download is only used after it has
been validated; otherwise the previous copy stays in use.
